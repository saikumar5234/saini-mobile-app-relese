Privacy Policy – Saini Dry Fruits Mobile App

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Saini Dry Fruits (“we”, “our”) operates the Saini Dry Fruits mobile application (Android). This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: mobile number and profile details you provide (e.g. name, GST number if entered).
  • Usage: interactions needed to show prices, history, categories, and notifications.
  • Device: push notification tokens if you allow notifications.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Display product prices, charts, history, and related content.
  • Send price or product update notifications (if enabled).
  • Improve reliability and security of the service.

4. Local storage on your device

The app may store recently loaded product data on your device so you can view it when offline. This data remains on your device until the app updates or clears it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed through our servers (api.sainidryfruits.com) to provide the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures such as HTTPS for data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your choices

You may update your profile in the app, disable notifications in device settings, or contact us to request account deletion or data questions.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
