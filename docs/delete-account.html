Request account deletion – Saini Dry Fruits

App name on Google Play: Saini Dry Fruits
Developer / business: Saini Mewa Stores (Saini Dry Fruits)
Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can request deletion of your Saini Dry Fruits app account and the personal data we hold about you in connection with that account. This page explains how to request deletion and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion (required steps)

Send us a deletion request using one of the methods below:

  1. Email support@sainidryfruits.com with the subject line: Account deletion request – Saini Dry Fruits.
  2. In the message, include the 10-digit mobile number registered on your account (and your GST number if you signed up with GST).
  3. Optionally include your registered name to help us verify the correct account.
  4. We will confirm your request by reply to the same email address or by contacting you on the registered mobile number.
  5. After verification, we delete your account and associated personal data as described below.

Processing time: We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive confirmation when deletion is finished.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymize account-linked data on our servers, including:

  • Account identifiers (mobile number, GST number if stored)
  • Profile information (e.g. name)
  • Authentication credentials (e.g. password stored in hashed form)
  • Push notification tokens linked to your account
  • Usage or session records tied to your user account, where applicable

Data that may be kept

Type Reason Retention
Information we must keep for legal, tax, or regulatory obligations Compliance with applicable law As required by law (then deleted or anonymized when no longer required)
Aggregated or de-identified statistics that cannot reasonably identify you Service improvement and security Kept without personal identifiers
Backup copies Disaster recovery Removed when backups rotate, typically within 90 days of deletion from live systems

Data on your device

The app may store cached product data and login session data on your phone. Deleting your server account does not automatically remove this local data. To remove it from your device:

  • Open Settings → Apps → Saini Dry Fruits → Storage → Clear data (Android), or uninstall the app.
  • Use Logout in the app before uninstalling if you are still signed in.

Questions

For privacy or deletion questions, contact support@sainidryfruits.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
